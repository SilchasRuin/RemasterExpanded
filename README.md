### Differences from Tabletop:  
Pirate and Viking grant Maritime Lore, a lore focused on various water based creatures, instead of Sailing Lore.  
Pirate grants a bonus to Acrobatics checks and saving throws to Balance instead of ignoring difficult terrain and uneven ground.  
Viking grants a 10 foot bonus to speed while underwater instead of improving Swim checks.  
Mercenary Motivation grants a +1 to 2 skills of your choice and perception for any check rather than applying to all skill checks related to your job.  
A number of spells are added to the spell schools of Wizards if Remaster Feats and other optional dependencies are present.  
[Remember the Lost](https://2e.aonprd.com/Spells.aspx?ID=2420) only deals increased damage to specific enemies in the campaign. The enemies it works on have to be added individually. If you find an enemy that it should deal extra damage to per it's text, let me know and I can update it.
